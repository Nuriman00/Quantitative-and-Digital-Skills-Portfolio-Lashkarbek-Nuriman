Construction Cost Overrun Risk Analysis
Skills Demonstrated
Factor Analysis: Condensed 44 risk variables into 6 latent factors (26.6% variance explained; KMO = 0.767)
Regression Modeling: R² = 0.223; found supply/procurement issues (β = 0.172) and contract management (β = 0.080) to be the main drivers of cost overruns
Risk Scoring: Developed composite risk scores; classified 143 respondents into 5 tiers
Advanced Excel: Used matrix operations (MMULT, MINVERSE), array formulas, and correlation analysis
Key Finding
Experience is the most significant protective factor (β = -0.207) against cost overruns.
Tools
Microsoft Excel (no external statistical packages)
